FAQ

Common questions about context-first DMS

What is metadata-driven document management?

Instead of filing documents into folders, a metadata-driven system tags each document with what it actually is — client, project, document type, status — so it can be found, governed, and connected to related work regardless of where it's stored.

How is M-Files different from a generic file share like SharePoint or Box?

General-purpose file shares organize by folder location. M-Files organizes by what a document is and who it belongs to, layering governance and workflow on top of systems you already use — including SharePoint, Teams, and network drives — rather than replacing them outright.

Is M-Files ready for AI and tools like Microsoft Copilot?

Yes. Because documents carry structured context and permissions, AI tools like Copilot and M-Files' own assistant, Aino, can retrieve and reason over the right information without surfacing content a user shouldn't see.

Which industries does M-Files support out of the box?

M-Files ships with configurations for document-driven industries including accounting and audit, manufacturing, consulting, engineering, wealth management, energy and utilities, scientific research, and insurance brokerage.

How long does it take to get up and running?

Timelines depend on how many systems you're connecting and how complex your governance requirements are. A live demo is the fastest way to get a project-specific estimate for your organization.
