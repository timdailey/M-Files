Body copy

When information lives in many systems, context is lost and governance weakens. M-Files connects documents to the people, projects, and work they support.

Regular weight, ~16–17px, generous line-height — reads as a standard corporate SaaS sans (Helvetica/Arial/system-UI class), not a distinctive licensed typeface

Buttons & UI patterns

Get a Demo View Pricing Explore the Product Tour

Solid purple primary button, outline secondary button, gradient reserved for the one "delight" CTA. Rounded corners (~6px), no drop shadows, no rounded pill shapes.

Observed design principles

Corporate-safe, not expressive

Mostly white/off-white space, navy text, a single restrained purple accent. Reads enterprise-software-trustworthy over bold or playful.

Proof-heavy above the fold

Review-site logos and star ratings sit right under the hero headline, before any product explanation.

Logo wall as credibility

"Trusted by 6,000+ organizations" with a scrolling customer-logo strip appears immediately after the hero.

Repetition-heavy stat blocks

Big number + small label pattern (ROI %, hours saved, systems connected) used to sell outcomes over features.

Gradient is a treat, not wallpaper

The one gradient element on the whole page is the interactive demo CTA — everything else stays flat and neutral.

Dense mega-navigation

Deep multi-column dropdown menus (Platform, Solutions, Resources) signal a mature, feature-heavy enterprise product.
